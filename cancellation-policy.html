Booking & Cancellation Policy

Securing Your Booking

A payment is required at the time of booking to secure your reservation at Bay Bellerive:

Standard bookings (brunch and dinner): $20 per person deposit, which is deducted from your final bill on the day.

Bottomless brunch: $66 per person, paid in full at the time of booking. This covers your bottomless brunch package. Any additional items ordered on the day are charged separately.

Cancellations & Amendments

We understand plans change. If you need to cancel or amend your booking, please let us know as early as possible.

24 hours' notice or more: your deposit or prepayment will be refunded in full.

Less than 24 hours' notice, or no-show: your deposit or prepayment is non-refundable.

Changes to Party Size

Please let us know about any reduction in numbers at least 24 hours before your booking. For reductions made within 24 hours, the deposit or prepayment for each absent guest is non-refundable. We'll always do our best to accommodate increases in party size, subject to availability.

Late Arrivals

We hold tables for 15 minutes past your booked time. If you're running late, please give us a call. After 15 minutes without notice, your table may be released and the booking treated as a no-show. For bottomless brunch, the session ends at the scheduled time, regardless of arrival.

Cancellations by Bay Bellerive

If we need to cancel or change your booking for any reason, your deposit or prepayment will be refunded in full.
